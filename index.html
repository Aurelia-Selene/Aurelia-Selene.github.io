Core Mechanics

  • Cracking is a Cybercrime (10th of Crimes 2.0) — brute-force + guess to break Torn's weak ICE (Individual Character Encryption) passwords.
  • Passwords are 4–10 chars; advanced targets add 1–9 encryption layers that must be brute-forced off before characters can be guessed.
  • Brute-forcing = 7 nerve/attempt — each success reveals 1 char or breaks 1 encryption layer.
  • Cracking (final step) = 5 nerve/attempt — only once every char is revealed; success pays money, critical failure = job lost + jail 3h16m–6h23m.
  • Guessing (unencrypted chars only): up to 3 wrong guesses per password before lockout.
  • Rig comes online with 1+ PSU and ≥100,000 MIPS; power budget = PSUs × 750W.
  • Chassis are 5×5, unlocked at Cracking skill 25 / 50 / 75 (starts as an offline computer, BFS 1).
  • 28 target types; 7–26 jobs spawn daily at midnight TCT and roll over if progress was made.
  • Honors: CRYPTOGRAPHER @ CS100 · CHARACTER ASSASSINATION @ 250 chars guessed.

eCPU = cheap, low heat, weakest OC. CPU = balanced, high heat. HPCPU = best MIPS/W & OC, hottest. Computer Fan (5W) = broad-area. Water Block = strong, confined. Heat Sink = adjacent only. PSU = 750W, 2 slots (rotatable), moderate heat. Fan / Water Block / Heat Sink are overheat-immune.

Parts work across the whole rig (a PSU in one chassis powers CPUs in another); heat stays per-chassis, and a slot's heat carries to the next part you install there.

Brute-Force Strength (BFS)

BFS = 14.069 × ln(MIPS) − 89.410 × √ln(MIPS) + 142.386 (≈ ±0.05, ln = natural log)

Higher BFS = more brute-force cycles per attempt = faster cracking. Extra MIPS raise BFS with diminishing (logarithmic) returns — so more MIPS ≠ proportionally faster.

Torn does not publish the exact formula; this is fitted to observed rig values (100k–3M MIPS).

Heat & Overheating

  • 0–50: Normal (safe).
  • 50–99: Hot (red aura) — still works, but can't be removed until it drops below 50%.
  • 100+: OVERHEAT — stops working until it cools below 100% (not lost).
  • Heat can reach 150%; decays naturally 1% / 60s. A fully-overheated part takes 2h30m to cool.
  • Heat is per-chassis; a removed part's heat stays in the slot and carries to the next part installed there.
  • Heat Sinks & Water Blocks are overheat-immune — they keep cooling regardless of their own heat.

CMT2570 (Fundamentals of Computer Architecture) reduces heat generated by all components by 25%.

Cooling

  • Computer Fan (5W): broad area (~3 tiles — adj −3, 2-tile −6, 3-tile −9 to −12). Cheapest, overheat-immune.
  • Water Block: confined, strong (1 tile); survives 8 HPCUs. Overheat-immune.
  • Heat Sink: adjacent only (removes ~90% of a neighbor's heat). Overheat-immune.

Overclocking

  • Set by dragging the power gauge into the orange/red zone; steps of 10% (max 150%). Affects CPUs only — fan draw & PSU heat are unaffected.
  • OC raises power + heat for all CPUs in proportion to the rate. HPCPUs gain more MIPS per % than eCPUs (eCPUs scale lowest).
  • Unlocks: CMT2128 → 30% OC · CMT2129 → 50% OC (max). CMT2570 is the heat-reduction course, not an OC unlock.

Rig Classifications

  • Zero-Heat — all components at 0 (full cooling coverage).
  • Low-Heat — <50 on all, 0 overheated.
  • Mid-Heat — 50–99 on some, 0 overheated.
  • High-Heat — some at 100+ (few uses before overheat).
  • Also classified by OC (pre/post) & chassis count (1–3).

Rig Design Patterns

  • eCPU-centric — many eCPUs, low power, ideal for zero/low-heat. (ex. ~535k MIPS @ 720W/750W)
  • HPCU-centric — fewer HPCUs, high MIPS, heavy cooling. (ex. ~716k MIPS @ 2140W/2250W)
  • Standardized — repeat one proven layout across multiple chassis; balanced.
  • Power Bank — dedicate a chassis to PSUs for OC headroom; others process.

Skill-Growth Paths

  • Slow / efficient — guess or brute-force once, then solve with minimal attempts. Feeds Character Assassination.
  • Quick / CS-farm — brute-force everything, no guessing. Fast daily CS, unlocks harder jobs sooner.

Honors & Merits

  • CRYPTOGRAPHER — reach CS100.
  • CHARACTER ASSASSINATION — guess 250 password characters.
  • All cracking = cybercrime offenses; max medal = 10,000 offenses (~60–70k nerve).

Education (Cracking track)

Key Formulas

  • BFS = 14.069 × ln(MIPS) − 89.410 × √ln(MIPS) + 142.386
  • Total MIPS = Σ component MIPS (after OC)
  • Total Power = Σ component draw (after OC) ≤ budget
  • Heat = (base × OC) − Σ cooling(adjacent)
  • Heat radiates between adjacent components.
